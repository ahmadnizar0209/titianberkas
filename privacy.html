Privacy Policy

TitianBerkas does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Files move directly between your Mac and your Android phone over USB. File names, folders and the record of transfers and edits are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's own container, and that record is encrypted.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.
  • Folders on your Mac are used only after you choose them in an Open or Save panel.
  • A support export is made only when you ask for it, holds only the event details you select, and is saved where you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • The Full Version is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the purchase; the developer receives no personal or payment information.
