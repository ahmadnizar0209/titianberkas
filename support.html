Support

Help with TitianBerkas.

The phone isn't found

Unlock the phone and connect it with a data-capable USB cable. In the phone's USB notification, choose File Transfer, not Charge only. Try another cable or port, and close other apps that use the phone, such as Android File Transfer or OpenMTP. Then choose Refresh and Connect.

The cable came out during a copy

Open Recovery. Choose Check on Phone… to see whether the change reached the phone, Save Copy to Mac… to keep the file, and Mark Reviewed… when you are done. The app never redoes a change by itself.

Edit a file that is on the phone

Select it in the Storage Browser and choose Actions › Edit With…. Save in your editor, choose Capture Saved Version in Managed Edits, then Save Back.

Daily limit

Basic allows three connection sessions a day with one phone. The Full Version has no daily limit.

Purchases

To restore the Full Version on another Mac, open Plans › Restore Purchases. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
