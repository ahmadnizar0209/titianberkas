TitianBerkas

Move files between your Mac and your Android phone over USB.

What it does

Connect an Android phone with a USB cable and browse its storage, including an SD card. Copy files to your Mac or to the phone, create folders, rename, move and delete. Every change is reviewed before it starts and checked afterwards by reading it back.

Edit phone files on your Mac

Open a phone file in any Mac app, save it, and send it back to the phone. Once the new version is verified, the copies on your Mac are removed.

Safe when things go wrong

If the cable is unplugged in the middle of a change, Recovery shows what was interrupted, lets you check the phone and keeps a copy you can save.

Free and Full Version

Basic is free: three connection sessions a day with one phone. The one-time Full Version In-App Purchase adds bulk operations, folder transfers, editing and several phones, with no daily limit. No subscription.
